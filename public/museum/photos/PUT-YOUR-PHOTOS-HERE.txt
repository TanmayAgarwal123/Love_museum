Put your photos in this folder (JPG, PNG or WEBP). The walls hold up to 41.

They are hung in FILE-NAME order and spread through all four rooms,
so name them:
  01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg ... 25.jpg ...

Then (optional) write a title / date / caption for each one in
  src/content/museum.config.ts  (the "photos" section, matched by file name).

Big photos are fine: web-sized copies are made automatically.
iPhone photos (.HEIC) won't show in browsers - export them as JPG first.
