Put up to 12 photos in this folder (JPG, PNG or WEBP).

They are hung on the museum walls in FILE-NAME order, so name them:
  01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg ... 12.jpg

Then write a title / date / caption for each one in
  src/content/museum.config.ts  (the "photos" section, matched by file name).

iPhone photos (.HEIC) won't show in browsers - export them as JPG first.
