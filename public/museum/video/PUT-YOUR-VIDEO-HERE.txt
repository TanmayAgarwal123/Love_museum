Put ONE video in this folder. It plays on the cinema screen when you two
sit on the bench.

  - Format: MP4 (H.264). iPhone .MOV files often won't play in Chrome or
    Android; export / convert to MP4 first.
  - Size: keep it under ~90 MB (GitHub rejects files over 100 MB).
    A 1-3 minute clip at 720p or 1080p is perfect.
  - Any file name works, e.g. our-video.mp4
