Put your videos in this folder (3-4 is perfect, up to 8). They play one
after another on the cinema screen when you two sit on the bench, with
Previous / Next buttons.

  - Name them in the order you want: 01.mp4, 02.mp4, 03.mp4, 04.mp4
  - Give each a title in src/content/museum.config.ts ("videos" section).
  - Format: MP4 (H.264). iPhone .MOV files often won't play in Chrome or
    Android; export / convert to MP4 first.
  - Size: under ~90 MB EACH (GitHub rejects files over 100 MB). Smaller
    (20-50 MB) starts faster on a phone. 720p or 1080p is plenty.
